The Idea

The bare minimum,
done right.

Most jobs don't need a ten ton grip truck and a crew of six. They need enough gear to get the shot right, without paying for the overhead, insurance paperwork, and padded contracts a full scale package comes with.

Indiglow Lighting exists for that gap. One compact half-ton package with all the basic grip gear you actually use, plus lighting units you add on when the job calls for it. We know indie budgets are tight, that's the whole idea: get more done for less. No insurance requirements, no long contracts, no ridiculous fees, and every order is delivered straight to your shooting location. Add us to your call sheet and we follow schedule and location changes like the rest of your crew. Just book us and we'll be there with everything you need.
