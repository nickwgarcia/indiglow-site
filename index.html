The Idea

Everything you need,
nothing you don't.

Most small productions don't need a ten-ton grip truck and a crew of six. They need dependable gear that gets the shot right, without paying for the overhead a full-scale package brings.

That's what our one-ton package is built for: everything most small productions need, plus lighting units you can add when the job calls for it. We think of it as resourcefulness, getting the most out of the right tools instead of hauling around gear you'll never touch. If a job calls for something more specialized, we're equipped to handle it; just tell us what you need and we'll source it.
